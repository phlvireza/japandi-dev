This page explains how japandi.dev handles information about the people who visit it.

Information collected on this site

This site is a set of static pages. It includes no analytics, no advertising, no tracking scripts, no embedded third-party content, and no contact form, and it sets no cookies. It makes no requests to any other domain. No account is created, and nothing is submitted through these pages.

Contacting me by email

The contact links on this site open your own email application. If you choose to write, your message and your email address reach me the way any ordinary email does, and I use them only to reply to you.

Web server logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider and any content delivery network serving these pages may record standard request information such as IP address, timestamp, requested URL, and browser user-agent string. This section must be confirmed against those providers' own documentation and retention practices before launch.

My projects
