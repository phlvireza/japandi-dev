Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This policy explains how Japandi Dev handles information when you visit japandi.dev or contact Japandi Dev by email.

Information collected when you visit

Japandi Dev is a static website. It has no user accounts, contact forms, advertising, third-party embeds, or tracking scripts. Japandi Dev does not use analytics and does not create a visitor profile.

The site is delivered through Cloudflare and hosted by Biznet Gio. To deliver and protect the site, these providers may process standard technical request data, including your IP address, request date and time, requested page, referrer, browser or device information, and security-related events. Japandi Dev uses this processing only to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

Cookies and browser storage

The pages on japandi.dev do not set cookies or store information in your browser. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie if one of its security features is triggered. Japandi Dev does not use that cookie for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Information you send by email

Email links on this site open your own email application. If you contact Japandi Dev, the information you choose to include—such as your email address, name, and message—is used to respond to you, manage the conversation, and address any related security or legal issue. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is not needed for your request.

How information is shared

Japandi Dev does not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed with:

  • Cloudflare, which delivers and protects the site;
  • Biznet Gio, which hosts the site;
  • the email service providers involved when you choose to send an email; and
  • authorities or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or investigate abuse.

Cloudflare operates a global network, so technical request data may be processed outside your country. Each service provider handles information under its own privacy terms.

Retention

Japandi Dev does not maintain its own analytics or visitor-log database. Cloudflare and Biznet Gio retain technical data according to their own service settings and policies. Email correspondence is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the conversation, security, record-keeping, or legal obligations, and is then deleted when no longer needed.

Your choices and requests

You may ask whether Japandi Dev holds personal information from an email conversation, and request access, correction, or deletion. A request may be limited where retaining information is necessary for security, record-keeping, or a legal obligation. You can also contact Cloudflare or Biznet Gio directly about information they process under their policies.

Other Japandi Dev projects

This policy covers japandi.dev only. Applications published by Japandi Dev, including Squirio, have separate privacy policies describing their own data practices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy and its new revision date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@japandi.dev.
