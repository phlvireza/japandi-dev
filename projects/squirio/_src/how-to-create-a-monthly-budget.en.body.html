Calculate dependable take-home income, reserve fixed obligations, set realistic flexible limits, fund savings, and leave a buffer. Use your payday-to-payday cycle when a calendar month does not match how money arrives.

Use recent income and spending records

Collect one to three months of transactions before setting limits. Separate dependable income from irregular income, then identify fixed commitments, flexible essentials, goals, and optional spending. If records are incomplete, begin with estimates and replace them as you track.

Build the budget in five steps

  1. Choose the period: calendar month or payday cycle.
  2. Enter conservative take-home income.
  3. Reserve rent, installments, bills, and other fixed commitments.
  4. Assign limits to food, transport, household costs, and discretionary categories.
  5. Fund savings and keep a buffer for uneven expenses.

Use a percentage rule only when it fits your income and expenses. Allocate savings and a buffer before assigning the remaining amount.

Review the completed period

Compare planned and recorded totals at the end of the period. When one category repeatedly exceeds its limit, change that limit for the next period. Use daily expense records as the basis for the change.
